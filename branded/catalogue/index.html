Foundations (all proposals)

Spacing, grid, radii, elevation

Spacing4px base scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Section padding clamp(48px, fluid, 96px).
GridContent max 1240px with fluid gutters (20 to 40px); the Toolkit mock shows roughly 89% content width at 1440. Cards run 3 across desktop, 2 at 960px, 1 at 640px.
Radii0 everywhere. The identity is square-cornered and isometric; the wireframe's pill filters and rounded listing card become square here (see FINDINGS).
ElevationNo blur shadows. Hover lifts use a solid offset slab in Green (an isometric extrusion), 6px by 6px.
ImageryNo photography in this preview. The supplied isometric backgrounds stand in, and every image placeholder carries the green overlay radiating from the top left (Toolkit p3).
Focus3px outline, 2px offset: Slate on light grounds, Green on dark grounds.
