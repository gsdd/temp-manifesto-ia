MGA website IA v3: navigation wireframe

Interactive mock of docs/01-primary-navigation.md and docs/02-sitemap.md. Labels, subtitles, order and URLs match the documents exactly. If this page and the documents disagree, the documents win.

The What we do panel is open when the page loads so the three pillars of the Growth Architecture triangle are the first thing you see: Growth Strategy, Activation Services, CEO Advisory. Move the cursor away, press Escape or click outside to close it; hover over What we do to reopen it. Links do not navigate; a message shows the URL they would open on the live site.

v3 is a wording pass. The structure is unchanged from v2. Every mega-nav item now has a plain-English subtitle carrying the words a prospect would search (customer experience, website, research, loyalty, membership, AI, operating model, interim, retainer). Three services are renamed from Andy's deck to the searched term; the deck names stay on the service pages. Reasons per label: docs/nav-wording-decisions.md.

Behaviour: hover opens after a short delay and closes after a short delay when the cursor leaves. Click on a top-level label opens the menu here; on the live site click also navigates to the landing page. Only one menu is open at a time. Our work is a plain link. Sectors are not in the header: they are in the footer only.

What this frame shows

  • Header: logo left, Contact button right and always visible, Menu control.
  • What we do accordion headed "Our Growth Architecture" with the three pillars in desktop order: Growth Strategy (open by default), Activation Services, CEO Advisory (quieter). Every item carries the same plain-English subtitle as desktop. Then one row of two links: All services, Expertise.
  • Our work: plain link. Insights: latest, reports, events, then Expertise with the five theme links. About: About Manifesto, Our team, How we work, Values and culture, Careers. Contact as a plain link.
  • Below the primary items, a quieter Who we work with block with the four sector links.
  • Toggle pattern used here: label navigates, separate plus or minus toggles. The nav doc allows a whole-row toggle with a View all link as the alternative; pick one in wireframes.
  • The panel scrolls if content exceeds the viewport. Header stays pinned.
