MGA website IA: navigation wireframe

Interactive mock of docs/01-primary-navigation.md and docs/02-sitemap.md. Labels, order and URLs match the documents exactly. If this page and the documents disagree, the documents win.

Hover over or click What we do, Our work, Insights or About in the desktop tab. Links do not navigate; a message shows the URL they would open on the live site. Press Escape to close any menu.

Behaviour: hover opens after a short delay and closes after a short delay when the cursor leaves. Click on a top-level label opens the menu here; on the live site click also navigates to the landing page. Only one menu is open at a time. Sectors are not in the header: they appear as a text link in the mega-nav bottom strip and in the footer.

What this frame shows

  • Header: logo left, Contact button right and always visible, Menu control.
  • What we do accordion with three sub-groups in desktop order: Growth Strategy (open by default), Activation Services, Expertise. Then two plain links: All services, CEO Advisory: Side-by-Side.
  • Our work: link plus filter list. Insights: link plus content types and themes. About: About Manifesto, Our team, How we work, Careers. Contact as a plain link.
  • Below the primary items, a quieter Who we work with block with the four sector links.
  • Toggle pattern used here: label navigates, separate plus or minus toggles. The nav doc allows a whole-row toggle with a View all link as the alternative; pick one in wireframes.
  • The panel scrolls if content exceeds the viewport. Header stays pinned.

Source: docs/02-sitemap.md. Weight is shown as a badge. Curly braces mark CMS-generated slugs; "(n)" means many pages of that type. Activation services are shown under the Activation group for readability but their URLs are flat under /services/.
