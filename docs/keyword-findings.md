# Keyword findings (UK) - Manifesto Growth Architects service pages

Source: DataForSEO Google Ads Search Volume + DataForSEO Labs Keyword Suggestions 
Location: United Kingdom (location_code 2826) · Language: English 
Date: 16 Sep 2026 
Approx. API cost: ~$0.32 (search volume + 16 suggestion tasks)

## How to read this

Exact Manifesto product phrases are often thin (tens of searches/month). That is normal for senior B2B consultancy. The useful signal is **adjacent demand**: phrases buyers type that should appear in H1/H2s, strand subtitles, and body copy - without renaming the nav to chase volume.

Volumes are average monthly Google Ads search volume for UK.

## By service page / cluster

### Growth Strategy (lead offer)
- Best primary: **growth strategy** (~480). Exact **growth strategy consultancy / consultant** ~70 each.
- Strong adjacent: **brand strategy** (~1,600), **brand strategy consultancy / consulting** (~390), **go to market strategy** (~880).
- Note: Volume supports a clear Growth Strategy page; body/H2s can borrow brand / GTM language where true to the offer. Do not retitle the nav to Brand Strategy.

### Proposition Innovation
- Exact **proposition design** ~20; **proposition innovation** no usable volume.
- Best commercial adjacent: **value proposition design** (~140); broader **value proposition** ~3,600 (noisy / mixed intent including EVP).
- Recommendation: Keep Manifesto label in nav. On-page H2s and intro should say value / proposition design in plain words so search can see it.

### Customer Research and Insight
- Exact **customer research agency** ~20; **customer insight consultancy** ~0.
- Strong method/buyer language: **customer research techniques** (~880), **customer research methods / companies** (~720), **customer journey mapping** (~2,900).
- Recommendation: Page title stays Customer Research and Insight. Lead H2s with methods, journey mapping, and insight outcomes - not only the consultancy noun.

### Experience Engineering (CX / websites)
- Exact **experience engineering** ~90; **cx consultancy** ~70; **website design consultancy** ~70; **experience design agency** ~110.
- Very strong adjacent (use as section language, not the nav rename): **customer journey mapping** (~2,900), **customer experience journey mapping** cluster (~2,900), UX/experience design phrases (thousands - noisy, agency-wide).
- Recommendation: Keep Experience Engineering label + strand subtitle "Customer experience and websites". Structure the page with H2s for journey mapping, CX, and websites (Dayinsure / Key Group pattern).

### AI Agents for Marketing
- Exact **ai agents for marketing** ~70; **ai marketing agents** ~390; bare **ai agents** ~9,900 (huge, generic / technical - poor sole target).
- Recommendation: Target the marketing-qualified phrases. Use AgentLab and "for marketing" in title/H1/meta. Do not rely on bare "AI agents" alone.

### Operating Model Design
- Exact **operating model design** ~140; **operating model** ~880; **operating model consultancy** ~10.
- Strong adjacent: **target operating model** (~1,600).
- Recommendation: Strongest volume case in Activation after journey/CX. H1/H2 can include operating model / target operating model language.

### Growth Office (interim growth team)
- Exact **growth office** ~10 (and Labs suggestions are mostly irrelevant "Post Office" noise).
- Better adjacent: **interim cmo** (~110 to 140). **interim growth director** / **transformation programme office** had no usable volume in this pull.
- Recommendation: Keep Growth Office as Manifesto label; strand subtitle and body must say interim / embedded growth team (and interim CMO where accurate). Do not SEO-target "growth office" as the primary phrase.

### AI Enablement
- **ai enablement** ~170; **ai transformation consultancy** ~10; **ai adoption consultancy** no volume in this pull.
- Recommendation: Thin but real niche. Page can own "AI enablement" as primary; supporting copy for adoption / skills / operating change.

### CEO Advisory (Side-by-Side)
- Exact **ceo advisory** ~10; **executive advisor** ~30; **board advisor** ~90; **ceo coach** ~320.
- Recommendation: Nav stays CEO Advisory. Body and meta can use advisor / board / coaching language carefully (coaching is adjacent, not identical to Side-by-Side). Low search demand - this page is relationship/proof led, not volume led.

## Cross-cutting recommendations for the IA

1. **Services remain the spine.** Volume is highest on problem/method phrases (journey mapping, operating model, value proposition, brand strategy) that belong on service pages as H2s and intro copy - not as extra top-nav items.
2. **Strand subtitles earn their keep.** Growth Office → interim growth team; Experience Engineering → customer experience and websites; these map to real search language. This pass extends a short subtitle to every strand.
3. **Do not rename the triangle for volume.** Proposition Innovation, Growth Office, Experience Engineering, CEO Advisory are brand/offer labels. Search-friendly language sits on the page.
4. **Activation group page** can mention journey mapping, operating model, and interim leadership in the "which of the six" lines to catch problem-minded searchers who land on the hub.
5. **Expertise themes** (Loyalty etc.) were not fully keyworded in this pass; do a second pass before locking theme titles if needed.
