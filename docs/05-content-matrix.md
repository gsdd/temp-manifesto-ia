# 05. Content matrix

Which content types live on which pages. Read this alongside `03-page-layouts.md` (block order and rules) and `04-canonicals-and-seo.md` (ownership).

---

## Key

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Primary home. This page owns the content type. The full version lives here and is edited here. |
| **S** | Surfaced. The page shows a summary, card or excerpt pulled from the primary home, always linking back to it. Usually automatic via tags. |
| **T** | Teaser. A one-paragraph or one-link mention, hand-written, pointing to the primary home. |
| blank | Not present on this page type. |

Notes on reading the matrix:

- A column with many **P** marks is a content-heavy page (Service detail, Case study, Insight). A column with mostly **S** and **T** is a router (Home, hubs, sector landings). This is by design: routers stay light and the weight sits on the canonicals and proof.
- Sector landing has no **P** except its own short intro. That is the practical definition of "light".
- Expertise theme has one **P** (the point of view). Everything else on it is surfaced. That keeps themes as a real dimension without duplicating services.
- CEO Advisory is a Service detail but is listed separately to show that it surfaces advisor profiles and uses a quieter CTA.

## 3. Content type by weight

How much of each content type the site needs at launch, so the pages above are not thin.

| Content type | Minimum at launch | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Service descriptions | 8 canonical + Activation group + CEO Advisory = 10 | Every service page in the nav must be complete on day one |
| Theme points of view | 5 | All five themes are in the mega-nav |
| Sector intros | 4 plus index | Footer links must land on something; noindex until threshold |
| Case studies | 12 minimum, tagged so that every service has at least one and every theme has at least two | Proof modules on services and themes must not be empty. Draw from the example client list in Source B. |
| Insights | 10 minimum, tagged so that every theme has at least two | Theme pages cannot publish with fewer than two |
| Team profiles | All leadership and all advisors, at least 100 words each | CEO Advisory depends on advisor profiles |
| Client logos | 12 to 16 with permissions | Home strip, About wall, sector landings |
| Client quotes | 4 to 6 | Case studies and How we work |
| FAQs | 3 to 6 per canonical service | Long-tail search |
| Methodology | 1 full page | Referenced from every service |

## 4. Ownership and editing

| Content type | Edited in | Appears via |
|---|---|---|
| Service, theme, sector, about, methodology copy | Its own page in the CMS | Direct |
| Case studies, insights, team profiles | Their own entries | Tags drive surfacing everywhere else |
| Cross-reference grid markers | Per service entry (which themes it applies to) | Rendered on Services hub and Expertise hub |
| Featured and pinned items | Per hub or per Home in the CMS | Curated blocks |
| Client logos and quotes | Asset library with client permission flag | Surfaced by tag or curation |
| Nav labels and footer | Global navigation settings | All pages |

Rule: if the same sentence appears on two pages, one of them is wrong. Fix it by turning the second into a link.
