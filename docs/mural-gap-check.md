## Open questions for Gary and Andy

1. **C and N members.** MURAL asks for a section. Is this a named network, a membership body, or an internal group? Placeholder on About and Our people until confirmed. Do not invent a sold service around it.
2. **Growth Collective.** Current-site nav item. Footer? A strand under Our thinking? Drop? Left out of the header on purpose.
3. **Events as a section versus a page.** Wireframe uses a section on `/insights/#events`. Split a page only if there is enough recap content to sustain it.
4. **Careers as first-class.** Chosen so the Contact split is honest and we stay at six items. Alternative: nest Careers back under About (five items plus Contact) and keep the Contact split by in-page links only. Sign-off needed; both are under seven.
5. **Our thinking versus Insights.** Label is Manifesto's current-site language. URL stays `/insights/` for now. Confirm the label; search language can stay in titles and H2s.
6. **Showreel at launch.** Wireframe assumes a still/visual at launch and film later, matching the sticky. Confirm.
7. **Email Mark tracking.** MURAL: keep but track which button emails Mark. Wireframe notes it on Work with us. Confirm it is still a required route.
8. **Trusted partner logos linking to cases.** MURAL: link only if a case exists for that logo. Dayinsure and Key Group are the only case shells; do not imply a case for Disney, Tate, Coty and the rest.
