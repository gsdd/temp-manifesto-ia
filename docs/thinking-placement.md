# Our thinking: placement decision

Gary: "all thinking at the bottom feels off." This note records what we changed on Home, and what we did not change in the chrome.

## Decision

**Our thinking stays a first-class header item** (six items, under the cap of seven). The library stays on `/insights/` with the type dropdown (Reports, Articles, Events and news, All thinking).

**On Home, thinking is no longer a strip above the closing CTA.** It sits **after the triangle (What we do) and before Our work**: one featured report plus one article, then "All thinking". That is still a teaser, not a reports grid.

## Why this place

Home has to answer, in order: who we are, what we do, that we have a point of view, that we can prove it. Putting thinking after the offer and before cases does that. Leaving it last made the page read as services, work, awards, themes, then an afterthought.

A full reports grid on Home is still out (MURAL: do not replicate Our thinking on Home). One featured piece plus one article is not that grid.

## What we did not do

- Did not add a seventh top-nav item.
- Did not nest thinking under What we do.
- Did not put CIVD, Life at Manifesto film, or a blogs list on Home.
- Did not change the Insights URL (`/insights/`).

## Shared pattern

The Home block is a teaser only. Expertise theme pages and the Our thinking hub keep their own lists. Thank-you can keep suggesting one insight as a next step. No second homepage-style thinking strip elsewhere.
