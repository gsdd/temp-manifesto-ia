# Andy's deck: coverage audit (v5, Part B)

Gary's worry (16 September): the Growth Architecture Services deck may not be fully reflected in the IA. This document re-reads the deck (Source B, "Manifesto's Growth Architecture Services", working draft Sept 26, confirmed current against the Drive file on 16 September) slide by slide and records where every pillar, service, named module, framing line and proof point lands in the v4 IA.

Status key:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Present** | Has a named home in v4 (nav, sitemap, a page template block, or Home) and the home is specified, not just implied |
| **Partial** | The idea is in v4 but the placement is vague ("available for body copy"), split, or missing an element of the slide |
| **Missing** | No home in v4 |

The right-hand column says what v5 does about it. The rule from the brief holds throughout: **nothing from the deck goes back into the mega-nav.** Gaps land as page modules, hub framing, sitemap notes or one quiet nav link, each with a reason.

Related: `competitor-nav-review.md` (Part A), `v5-refinements.md` (Part C), `00-sources.md` (Source B summary).
